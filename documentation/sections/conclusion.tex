\subsection{Lessons Learned}

%% GUIDE (3-4 bullets, reflective tone):
%%  - Choreography-based sagas eliminate the orchestrator bottleneck but require
%%    careful in-memory state management (pendingPayments Map); a production system
%%    should persist saga state to survive container restarts.
%%  - The PubSub utility's auto-reconnect and subscriber-restore pattern proved
%%    essential during development — transient RabbitMQ restarts no longer
%%    required manual service restarts.
%%  - Unifying on Node.js for all 13 services significantly reduced integration
%%    friction (single runtime, shared utility modules) compared to a polyglot
%%    microservices approach, at the cost of losing language-specific ecosystem benefits.
%%  - Durable queues and manual consumer ACKs are non-negotiable from day one;
%%    retrofitting these guarantees into an already-running system is far more
%%    disruptive than designing them in upfront.
